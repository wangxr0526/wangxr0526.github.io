# About

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Zhejiang University, working with Prof. Tingjun Hou. I completed my doctoral and master's research under the supervision of Prof. Xiaojun Yao. My main research interests are AI-assisted drug design and intelligent chemistry, with a particular focus on AI-assisted synthesis planning, enzyme catalysis-related prediction, and specialized scientific agents. My work integrates machine learning, cheminformatics, and bioinformatics to develop algorithmic models and application platforms for single-step retrosynthesis prediction, reaction condition recommendation, multistep synthesis planning, enzyme active site annotation, and enzyme function prediction. I have led projects including the Young Scientists Fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the National Postdoctoral Researcher Program, Category C. I have published <span id="total_pub_summary">39</span> SCI papers in journals including *Nat. Commun.*, *Chem. Rev.*, *Research*, *Chem. Eng. J.*, *J. Med. Chem.*, and *J. Chem. Inf. Model.*, including 11 first-author or co-first-author papers and 2 papers in Nature Portfolio journals; these works have received <strong id="total_cit">1100+</strong> citations.

# Research Interests

<div class="research-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### AI-Assisted Synthesis Planning
My research in AI-assisted synthesis planning focuses on reaction prediction, condition recommendation, multistep synthetic route design, and route plausibility evaluation. I aim to integrate chemical knowledge, experimental data, and intelligent algorithms to develop interpretable, generalizable, and practically useful synthesis planning methods for drug molecules, functional molecules, and complex organic compounds. This line of work also supports automated and intelligent synthesis platforms by connecting computational prediction, experimental decision-making, and outcome evaluation.
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### Enzyme Catalysis Prediction
My research in enzyme catalysis prediction and intelligent enzyme design focuses on enzyme function recognition, catalytic site analysis, enzymatic reaction matching, enzyme-substrate interaction modeling, catalytic mechanism analysis, and enzyme design and engineering. By combining biocatalysis knowledge, protein information, and data-driven methods, I aim to develop efficient and interpretable strategies for enzyme catalysis prediction, supporting enzyme function annotation, substrate screening, enzyme molecular design, and green synthesis applications.
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown="1">
### Specialized Agents for Synthesis Planning and Enzyme Catalysis
I am interested in developing specialized scientific agents for synthesis planning and enzyme catalysis. This direction focuses on the role of large language models in task understanding, knowledge integration, tool coordination, and result evaluation. The goal is to connect domain algorithms, chemical and biocatalytic knowledge, database resources, and computational tools to improve automated analysis and decision support for complex scientific problems.
</div>

# Tools and Platforms

- [EasIFA GitHub repository](https://github.com/wangxr0526/EasIFA) and [EasIFA2.0 online service](https://cadd.zju.edu.cn/easifa/): a multimodal deep learning system for enzymatic active site annotation.
- [ChemEnzyRetroPlanner GitHub repository](https://github.com/wangxr0526/ChemEnzyRetroPlanner) and [ChemEnzyRetroPlanner demo website](https://cadd.zju.edu.cn/retroplanner/): an intelligent planning platform for hybrid enzymatic-organic synthesis route design.
- [RetroPrime](https://github.com/wangxr0526/RetroPrime): a Transformer-based single-step retrosynthesis prediction algorithm inspired by chemists' retrosynthetic thinking.
- [Parrot](https://github.com/wangxr0526/Parrot): an autoregressive general reaction condition recommendation tool.
- [Egret](https://github.com/xiaodanyin/Egret): a contrastive learning-enhanced reaction yield prediction model.
- [CODD-PRED](https://codd.iddd.group/): an integrated platform for small-molecule target identification and bioactivity prediction.
- [ChemistGA](https://github.com/jkwang93/ChemistGA): a genetic algorithm-based framework for generating synthesizable molecules.
